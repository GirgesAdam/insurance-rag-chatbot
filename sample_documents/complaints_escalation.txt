Document: SecureLife Complaints and Escalation Procedure
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01

1. Complaint Definition
A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about a SecureLife product, service, claim decision, staff interaction, delay, or communication issue.

2. How to Submit a Complaint
Customers can submit complaints by email, customer portal, phone, branch office, or mobile app. The customer should include policy number, claim number if applicable, contact details, and a clear description of the issue.

3. Complaint Acknowledgment
SecureLife aims to acknowledge complaints within 2 business days of receipt.

4. Complaint Resolution Timeline
SecureLife aims to resolve standard complaints within 15 business days. Complex complaints may require additional time, and the customer should receive updates.

5. Escalation Levels
Level 1: Customer Support Team
Level 2: Complaints Resolution Team
Level 3: Compliance and Management Review
Level 4: External regulator or dispute resolution body, where applicable

6. Claim Rejection Dispute
If a customer disagrees with a rejected claim, they may request a review. The customer should provide new evidence, missing documents, or reasons why they believe the policy terms were incorrectly applied.

7. Chatbot Handling Rule
The chatbot can explain the complaint process and required information. It should not criticize staff, admit legal liability, or promise compensation. It should offer escalation to a human agent.

8. Urgent Complaints
Urgent complaints involving medical emergencies, vehicle accidents, legal deadlines, or vulnerability should be escalated to a human agent immediately.
