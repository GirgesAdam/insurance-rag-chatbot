Document: SecureLife Motor Comprehensive Insurance Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Product code: MOTOR-COMP-2026

1. Product Summary
SecureLife Motor Comprehensive Insurance provides coverage for accidental damage to the insured vehicle, third-party liability, theft, fire, natural perils, and optional roadside assistance.

2. Eligibility
The policy is available for private passenger vehicles up to 10 years old at the start date of the policy. Vehicles used for racing, delivery platforms, commercial transport, or rental services require special approval and are not covered under the standard private motor plan.

3. Main Coverage
The Motor Comprehensive plan may cover:
- Accidental collision damage to the insured vehicle
- Vehicle theft following a police report
- Fire damage caused by accidental fire or external explosion
- Third-party property damage up to the policy limit
- Third-party bodily injury according to applicable law
- Natural perils including flood, storm, and hail, if listed on the policy schedule
- Glass breakage for windscreen and windows
- Towing to the nearest approved repair center after an insured accident

4. Optional Add-ons
The following add-ons are available if shown in the policy schedule:
- Roadside assistance
- Replacement car benefit up to 7 days
- Agency repair for vehicles under 3 years old
- Personal accident benefit for driver and passengers
- No-claim discount protection

5. Standard Deductible
The standard deductible is the amount paid by the customer when an approved own-damage claim is repaired. The exact deductible is shown in the policy schedule. If the accident is caused by an identified third party and recovery is successful, the deductible may be refunded.

6. Exclusions
The policy does not cover:
- Driving without a valid license
- Driving under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, or prohibited substances
- Using the vehicle for racing, drifting, speed testing, or illegal activity
- Wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, electrical failure, or maintenance issues
- Tire damage unless it occurs with other covered accidental damage
- Damage caused intentionally by the insured person
- Loss of personal belongings inside the vehicle unless an add-on is purchased
- Depreciation, loss of market value, or inconvenience
- Claims reported more than 30 days after the incident without acceptable reason

7. Claim Reporting
Motor accidents should be reported as soon as possible and preferably within 48 hours. Theft claims require a police report immediately after discovery. The customer should not authorize repairs before SecureLife inspects the vehicle or approves the repair center.

8. Required Documents for Motor Claims
For accident damage:
- Policy number
- Driving license of the driver
- Vehicle registration card
- Police report or accident report
- Photos of vehicle damage
- Repair estimate if requested

For theft claims:
- Policy number
- Police report
- Vehicle registration card
- Original vehicle keys if available
- Theft declaration form

9. Repair Network
SecureLife may direct customers to an approved repair center. Agency repair is only available if the policy schedule includes agency repair coverage.

10. Important Disclaimer
This summary does not replace the full policy wording. Final claim approval depends on policy terms, exclusions, documents, and investigation results.
