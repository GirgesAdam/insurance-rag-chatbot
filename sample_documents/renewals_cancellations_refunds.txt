Document: SecureLife Renewals, Cancellations, and Refunds
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01

1. Policy Renewal
Customers may renew eligible policies before the expiry date through the customer portal, mobile app, branch office, or approved agent. Renewal terms may change based on claims history, age, underwriting rules, market conditions, or changes in coverage.

2. Renewal Reminder
SecureLife normally sends renewal reminders 30 days and 7 days before policy expiry. Failure to receive a reminder does not extend the policy unless confirmed in writing.

3. Grace Period
Some products may include a grace period if stated in the policy schedule. A grace period does not automatically mean claims are covered. Coverage during the grace period depends on product rules and premium payment status.

4. Cancellation by Customer
Customers may request cancellation by submitting:
- Policy number
- Cancellation request form
- Customer identity verification
- Bank account details for refund if applicable
- No-claim declaration if requested

5. Cancellation by Company
SecureLife may cancel a policy for reasons including non-payment, fraud, misrepresentation, or breach of policy terms. Written notice will be provided according to policy rules.

6. Refund Rules
Refunds depend on the product, time used, claim history, and policy terms. If a claim has been paid or is under review, refund eligibility may be reduced or declined. Administration fees may apply.

7. Motor Policy Cancellation
Motor insurance cancellation may require proof that the vehicle was sold, exported, deregistered, or insured with another provider. Local regulatory rules may apply.

8. Health Policy Cancellation
Health policy cancellation may be subject to underwriting and regulatory requirements. If medical claims were paid during the policy period, refund may not be available.

9. Travel Policy Cancellation
Travel policies may be cancelled before the trip start date if no claim has occurred. After the trip begins, cancellation refund is usually not available.

10. Important Note
The chatbot should not calculate final refund amounts. Refund calculations must be confirmed by SecureLife's policy administration team.
